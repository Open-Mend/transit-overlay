# Privacy policy

Transit Overlay for Home Search does not collect or send any data.

- It runs only on https://www.zillow.com/ pages.
- It makes no network requests of its own. Map layers (transit, bike lanes,
  traffic, places) come from the Google Maps library that Zillow's page
  already loads.
- To make the map faster, it trims some of Zillow's own map requests in your
  browser: off-market homes and property lines are removed from the map data
  Zillow receives, and with "Full map" on, Zillow is asked for map results
  only. Nothing extra is sent anywhere.
- Your settings and pins (their locations and labels) are saved in your
  browser's local storage on zillow.com and never leave your device.
- Google Maps opens in a new tab only when you click one of its links; that
  sends the spot or address you chose to Google, like any Google Maps link.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Open-Mend/transit-overlay/issues
