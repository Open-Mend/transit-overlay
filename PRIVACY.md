# Privacy policy

Transit Overlay for Home Search does not collect, store, or send any data.

- It runs only on https://www.zillow.com/ pages.
- It makes no network requests of its own. The transit lines come from the
  Google Maps library that Zillow's page already loads.
- Your Off/On choice is saved in your browser's local storage on zillow.com
  and never leaves your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Open-Mend/transit-overlay/issues
