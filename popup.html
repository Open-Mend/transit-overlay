Transit Overlay for Home Search

Map tools for Zillow search pages, by OpenMend

Map menu · click "Map" on the map

  • Transport: transit with station names, bike lanes, traffic
  • Places: businesses, medical, schools and more
  • Climate Risks: flood, fire, wind, air, heat
  • Tools: dark mode, hover highlight, hide unlisted homes, measure, Google Maps

On the map

  • Right-click to add a pin with optional walking rings, or open the spot in Google Maps. Drag pins to move; click to edit.
  • Full map (next to "Map") hides the listings.
  • Zoom in further than Zillow allows.

On listings

  • Hover a card to see where it is on the map.
  • "…" menu → Google Maps opens the address; click its photo for Street View.
Open Zillow

Collects no data; settings and pins stay in this browser.
