## Name
Transit Overlay for Home Search

## Short description (Chrome/Edge ≤132 chars, Firefox summary ≤250)
Adds a Transit option to the map menu on Zillow search pages, showing public transit lines and stations.

## Description
See public transit while you house hunt.

This extension adds a Transit option (Off / On) to the Map menu on Zillow
search pages. Turn it on to show subway, light rail and commuter rail
lines and stations on the map, using Google's public transit layer.

- Works with Satellite, Street view, Climate Risks and Schools
- Remembers your choice
- No account, no settings, no tracking

How to use: open a Zillow search, click "Map" at the bottom of the map, and
choose Transit → On.

Coverage depends on Google's transit data and is best in larger cities.

Privacy: collects no data and makes no network requests of its own.
Open source: https://github.com/Open-Mend/transit-overlay

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Zillow.

- Single purpose (Chrome): Shows public transit lines and stations on Zillow's search map.
- Permissions: none. Host access: runs only on https://www.zillow.com/* to add the
  Transit option to the map menu and draw the transit layer.

- Data collection: None. (Firefox manifest declares `data_collection_permissions: none`.)
